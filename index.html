Power Runners

Solar hardware and battery flow

For contributors helping the technical lead keep vendor tables, charging stations, and the microgrid stable.

Beginning

Set the system before guests arrive.

  1. Check charging stations and batteries.
  2. Move batteries to vendor areas.
  3. Connect batteries to the solar microgrid.
  4. Power vendor tables and test appliances.
Middle

Keep energy stable during the fair.

  1. Record system output and energy flow.
  2. Swap batteries when needed.
  3. Check extra batteries after breaks.
  4. Run a mid-day vendor energy survey.
End

Shut down cleanly and report back.

  1. Give microgrid checkups and short tours.
  2. Survey vendor tables at closing.
  3. Unplug the energy system safely.
  4. Submit numbers, inventory, and final notes.

Event Lead · Coordination Layer

Crew training, stage changes, and final accountability

For the person guiding the system: train the three operational crews, watch shift changes, coordinate vendor needs, and turn the day into a complete operations record.

Beginning

Train the crew and open the site.

  1. Run morning setup and crew training.
  2. Confirm event setup, gear, and tools are ready.
  3. Check canopy, table, chair, and infrastructure setup.
  4. Coordinate vendor setup support and final area checks.
Middle

Manage shifts and active changes.

  1. Monitor shift changes and crew handoffs.
  2. Handle stage or speaker-to-music switchovers.
  3. Coordinate parking and attendance updates.
  4. Keep lunch rotations covered without losing site support.
End

Close the operation and receive reports.

  1. Start infrastructure breakdown in the right order.
  2. Confirm vendor table breakdown and final area checks.
  3. Review final breakdowns and inventory cleanup.
  4. Receive submitted reports and close the day.
