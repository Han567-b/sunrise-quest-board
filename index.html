Character Roles

The three MVP roles from the project one-pager.

These are the roles the September test is actually built around. Each role gets a comic-book narrative, an onboarding character sheet, clear deliverables, and a review path after the event.

Solar / Technical

Power Runners

Manage energy distribution, solar hardware, battery readiness, cable safety, and power stability across the event site.

  • Best for: solar, electrical, equipment, safety
  • Proof: battery logs, setup photos, power notes
  • Reports through: technical lead and Captain's Log
Operations

Green Workers

Handle physical infrastructure: tent setup, site logistics, vendor support, material movement, and breakdown.

  • Best for: setup, logistics, tools, guest flow
  • Proof: setup checklist, handoff notes, final sweep
  • Reports through: event director and crew standups
Digital / Remote

Cloud Support

Support on-site teams from a distance with schedule updates, digital administration, documentation, and communication routing.

  • Best for: documentation, maps, comms, admin
  • Proof: support log, issue notes, uploaded records
  • Reports through: remote dashboard and status queue

Marketplace Model

Four marketplace patterns, rewritten for Sunrise.

This is where the platform research becomes product design. The page should feel familiar to people who have used service marketplaces, but the language stays focused on community work, verified contribution, and event readiness.

01

Fast task intake

Instead of making people read a long manual first, the board starts with role category, date, location, expected shift, and what needs to be done.

02

Guided comparison

Applicants are compared by fit, availability, proof, skill tags, and readiness, so leads can choose people with context instead of guessing.

03

Packaged skill identity

Player Cards package a contributor's skills, badges, prior work, and interests so they are discoverable for the right quest.

04

Milestone-based work

Every quest has checkpoints: accept, prepare, standup, execute, submit proof, review, and update reputation.

Reward Types

What the three reward filters mean.

These are not decoration. They decide what type of opportunity a contributor is looking for and how the completion will be recorded after the event.

Quest Credits

Internal contribution points. Credits show how much verified work someone has completed and can support future role recommendations.

Stipend Eligible

Marks quests that may qualify for paid support or program compensation. These roles usually need higher trust, clearer proof, and lead approval.

Badge Progress

Tracks skill growth. Completing a quest can unlock badges such as Solar 101, Site Safety, Crew Standup, or Documentation Support.
