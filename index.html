Core System

A recruiting board plus a hiring desk.

01

Recruit Contributors

New participants browse open quests, filter by skill, see the expected commitment, and apply with a Player Card instead of a blank form.

  • Quest categories for solar, operations, digital support, and media
  • Fit score based on skills, level, availability, and readiness
  • Role preview with reward, proof required, and support contact
02

Hire & Assign Teams

Organizers compare applicants, review verified history, shortlist contributors, and assign a Quest Party with clear milestones.

  • Applicant comparison by reliability, proof, notes, and role fit
  • Milestone handoffs before, during, and after the event
  • Completion review that updates badges, credits, and reputation

Marketplace Flow

How recruiting and hiring work together.

The system is designed for both sides of the event: contributors need an easy way to find meaningful work, and organizers need a structured way to choose the right people without losing context.

Contributor Side

Find a quest that fits.

  1. Create a Player Card with skills, interests, availability, and training proof.
  2. Browse recommended quests or search by role type, reward, and schedule.
  3. Apply with a short note explaining why the role fits your background.
  4. Complete milestones and build visible reputation after review.
Organizer Side

Post, compare, assign.

  1. Publish a quest with scope, time window, location, reward, and proof needed.
  2. Review applicants through Player Cards instead of scattered messages.
  3. Shortlist by trust signals, readiness, past work, and event-day availability.
  4. Assign the Quest Party and track completion through the Captain's Log.
