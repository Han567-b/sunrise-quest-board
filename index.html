Event-day timeline

Three role journeys for September 26.

Each role has a beginning, middle, and end so contributors know what to prepare, what to do during the fair, and what proof to hand back after the work is finished.

Power Runners

Solar hardware and battery flow

For contributors helping the technical lead keep vendor tables, charging stations, and the microgrid stable.

Beginning

Set the system before guests arrive.

  1. Check charging stations and batteries.
  2. Move batteries to vendor areas.
  3. Connect batteries to the solar microgrid.
  4. Power vendor tables and test appliances.
Middle

Keep energy stable during the fair.

  1. Record system output and energy flow.
  2. Swap batteries when needed.
  3. Check extra batteries after breaks.
  4. Run a mid-day vendor energy survey.
End

Shut down cleanly and report back.

  1. Give microgrid checkups and short tours.
  2. Survey vendor tables at closing.
  3. Unplug the energy system safely.
  4. Submit numbers, inventory, and final notes.

Green Workers

Site setup, guest flow, and breakdown

For contributors turning the site plan into a working fair space and resetting the field after closing.

Beginning

Build the fair layout.

  1. Check in with the operations lead.
  2. Unload tents, tables, signage, and supplies.
  3. Set up vendor and guest areas from the site plan.
  4. Confirm walkways, safety edges, and handoff points.
Middle

Keep the site moving.

  1. Run materials to teams that need support.
  2. Keep guest paths clear and tables stocked.
  3. Help leads solve small layout or supply issues.
  4. Record what changed from the original setup plan.
End

Reset the field after the fair.

  1. Break down tents, tables, signs, and stations.
  2. Sort reusable supplies from trash and missing items.
  3. Complete a final sweep with the operations lead.
  4. Submit photos and checklist notes.
Cloud Support

Remote updates, proof, and feedback records

For contributors supporting the field team through status notes, digital files, questions, and post-event learning.

Beginning

Open the digital desk.

  1. Confirm the day's role list and contact paths.
  2. Prepare folders for photos, logs, and proof.
  3. Share the status channel with on-site leads.
  4. Check that each crew has the right guide.
Middle

Route questions and updates.

  1. Track live updates from Power Runners and Green Workers.
  2. Route questions to the correct lead quickly.
  3. Organize photos, issue notes, and schedule changes.
  4. Flag missing proof before the day ends.
End

Turn the day into learning.

  1. Collect final reports and proof folders.
  2. Compare planned duties with what happened on site.
  3. Prepare feedback notes for the next comic guide.
  4. Close the record for credits and badge review.
